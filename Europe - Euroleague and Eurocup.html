Tool Guide ✖

RS3 Data

  1. Copy the official data from the Player Stats page.
  2. Paste the data into Online Excel.
  3. Perform the necessary formatting changes to achieve standard alignment.
  4. Copy the formatted data.
  5. Paste it into the RS3 Data (Excel) section.

Official Data

  1. Copy Official Team A data with headers and paste it into Official Team A.
  2. Copy Official Team B data with headers and paste it into Official Team B.

Compare

  1. Click Review Player Matches.
  2. A Player Mapping dropdown popup will appear.
  3. Review all player mappings carefully.
  4. Correct any incorrect mappings.
  5. Click Confirm Mapping.
  6. The comparison results will be displayed.
Follow these steps sequentially to ensure accurate boxscore comparison.
